A Three Minute Thesis presentation given at EUSIPCO 2026, covering how camera,
LiDAR, and radar contribute complementary strengths to occupancy grid mapping:
cameras supply visual priors that guide occupancy allocation, LiDAR adds
accurate range detection, and radar remains dependable in adverse weather while
its Doppler returns support probabilistic inter-frame consistency.
