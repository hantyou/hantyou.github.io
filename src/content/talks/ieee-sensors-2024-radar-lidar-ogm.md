This presentation of our IEEE SENSORS 2024 paper introduces a radar–LiDAR
fusion strategy for occupancy grid mapping in automotive driving. Occupied cells
in a driving scene are sparse, so the map is estimated within a sparse Bayesian
learning framework. Within that framework, measurements from the two sensors are
fused softly: LiDAR contributes accurate range detections, and radar contributes
robustness in conditions where optical sensing degrades.

The recording was published by the IEEE Sensors Council. The work was later
extended into a journal article,
[Spatial Sparsity-Aware Radar-LiDAR Fusion for Occupancy Grid Mapping in Automotive Driving](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2025.3592023)
(IEEE Sensors Journal, 2025).
