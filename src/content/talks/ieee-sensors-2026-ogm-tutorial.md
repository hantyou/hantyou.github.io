Occupancy grid mapping (OGM) is a fundamental building block in probabilistic,
spatial world models that support safe navigation and decision-making in both
autonomous vehicles and mobile robots. This tutorial provides a blend of OGM
theory and practice, connecting Bayesian estimation principles with modern
autonomy stacks.

We begin with classical formulations: inverse sensor and Bayesian models, and
then progress to learning based OGM. We then discuss extensions to dynamic
environments (moving objects, temporal filtering, and tracking in grid space)
and 3D/semantic grids for richer scene understanding. The session emphasizes
impact of anomalies in real-world sensor data from modalities like LiDAR and
radar used in OGM, and robustness in adverse conditions.

Participants will learn how occupancy grids interface with downstream modules
and how to evaluate mapping quality using metrics aligned with real-time
constraints and safety requirements. Overall, the tutorial offers both
conceptual insights and practical guidance for building robust occupancy-based
perception systems in real-world settings.
