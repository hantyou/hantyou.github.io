This research strand sits within SPEAR's multimodal sensing sub-project. It combines camera information with radar-based occupancy-grid mapping, using visual context to inform the prior beliefs used by the mapping method.

The related paper presents a camera-aided binary-prior approach for automotive occupancy-grid mapping.
