# Curriculum vitae

## Profile

**Peiyuan Zhai** — PhD Researcher, Autonomous Perception and Sensor Fusion

Delft, The Netherlands · [hantyou.zhai@gmail.com](mailto:hantyou.zhai@gmail.com)

PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at Delft University of Technology (expected February 2027), building probabilistic perception methods for autonomous vehicles. I develop occupancy grid mapping algorithms that fuse automotive radar, LiDAR, and camera detections using sparse Bayesian learning, and evaluate them on the nuScenes and RADIATE datasets.
